Working Memory · Daily Practice

Train your Working Memory
with daily N-Back.

A simple training tool for daily N-Back tasks, widely used in cognitive psychology research. Designed as a measurement instrument for tracking and consistency — not a brain-training game.

See Features

Features

Stripped of distractions — designed to focus on training and tracking.

01

Dual N-Back: Position + Audio

Check position on a 3×3 grid and spoken audio simultaneously. Follows the standard format used in research.

02

Daily Records & Charts

Visualize daily scores, accuracy rates, and N-level progression with Swift Charts. A measurement dashboard to support consistency.

03

Automatic iCloud Sync

SwiftData + CloudKit automatically syncs data across devices. Records carry over when you switch devices.

04

Game Center Support

3 leaderboards and 8 achievements. Compare with yourself — and with the world.

05

Zero Ads During Training

No ads are ever shown during training sessions — an iron rule to protect your focus.

06

13 Languages

The interface supports 13 languages and follows your device setting. Audio cues stay in English letters, the standard stimulus set used in research.
